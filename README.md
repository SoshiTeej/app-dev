# My Favorite Movies

These are some of my all-time favorite films that showcase incredible storytelling, action, and the thrill of racing.

---

## 1. Avengers: Endgame

**Release Year:** 2019  
**Director:** Anthony and Joe Russo  
**Genre:** Superhero, Action, Adventure

### Why I Love It

*Avengers: Endgame* is the epic conclusion to the Infinity Saga. The film brings together over a decade of storytelling and delivers an emotional, action-packed finale.

**Favorite Moments:**
- The "Avengers Assemble" scene
- Tony Stark's ultimate sacrifice
- The time heist adventure
- Cap wielding Mjolnir

> "I am Iron Man." – Tony Stark

---

## 2. Senna

**Release Year:** 2010  
**Director:** Asif Kapadia  
**Genre:** Documentary, Sports

### Why I Love It

This documentary beautifully captures the life and career of legendary Formula 1 driver **Ayrton Senna**. It's a raw and emotional journey through one of motorsport's greatest heroes.

***Key Themes:***
1. Passion and dedication
2. The price of greatness
3. Rivalry and sportsmanship

---

## 3. Ford v Ferrari

**Release Year:** 2019  
**Director:** James Mangold  
**Genre:** Biography, Drama, Sports

### Why I Love It

An incredible true story about determination, friendship, and the pursuit of perfection. The film follows Carroll Shelby and Ken Miles as they build a revolutionary race car to compete at Le Mans.

**Cast:**
- Matt Damon as Carroll Shelby
- Christian Bale as Ken Miles

> "I'm going to build you a car that will beat Ferrari." – Carroll Shelby

**What Makes It Special:**
- Stunning cinematography
- Authentic racing sequences
- Powerful performances
- The underdog story

---

## 4. F1 (Upcoming Movie)

**Expected Release:** 2025  
**Director:** Joseph Kosinski  
**Genre:** Action, Drama, Sports

### What I'm Excited About

Directed by Joseph Kosinski (*Top Gun: Maverick*) and starring **Brad Pitt**, this film promises to bring authentic Formula 1 racing to the big screen like never before.

**Why It's Exciting:**
- Real F1 circuits and teams
- Actual racing footage
- Brad Pitt plays a returning driver
- Collaboration with Mercedes-AMG Petronas F1 Team
